Mentions Behind the Score

Method and coverage

Scoring Scale & Mix

Every qualifying organic mention is scored from -100 (strongly negative) to +100 (strongly positive). The overall score and each channel score are the rounded mean of mentions in that bucket (blank/— when 0 mentions appear).

  • Positive: score > +10
  • Neutral: score between -10 and +10
  • Negative: score < -10

Channels & Noise Filtering

Coverage spans four public buckets: News & blogs (tech press, Dev.to, Medium, Habr, engineering blogs), Dev communities (Reddit, Hacker News, Stack Overflow, GitHub), Review sites (G2, Capterra, TrustRadius, Product Hunt), and Social & video (X, Bluesky, LinkedIn, YouTube). Official corporate accounts, vendor SEO/marketing posts, job listings, spam, and pure how-to tutorials without sentiment are excluded.

Confidence Thresholds

  • Low: 1–3 mentions or low-engagement posts
  • Medium: 4–12 mentions across at least 2 distinct sources
  • High: 13+ mentions or high-engagement community threads
